# The Price of Protein: Global Bioavailable Protein Affordability & Spatial Market Atlas (v1.3)

Empirical harmonization pipeline, metropolitan case-study explorer (`90` global metros, `959` real geolocated sales locations across four market tiers), SHA-256 verification manifest, and camera-ready manuscripts (*Scientific Data* & *Nature Food*).

## Key Empirical Artifacts
- **`public/data/metro_case_studies.json`**: `90` globally stratified metropolitan areas and `959` empirical outlets (`Wholesale`, `Supermarket`, `Wet Market`, `Peri-Urban Feeder`) with DIAAS-adjusted cost (`u_usd`) and P20/median labor minutes (`labor_min_p20`, `labor_min_med`).
- **`public/data/sha256_manifest.json`**: Cryptographic SHA-256 checksums for all published data and manuscript artifacts.
- **`public/papers/`**: Camera-ready manuscripts (`paper1_scientific_data.pdf`, `paper2_nature_food.pdf`) and Supplementary Information (`SI_1.pdf`).
